01 / Gameplay concept

The complete picture

One frame brings together the battlefield, five emplacement positions, helicopter, infantry, light armor and the proposed HUD.

This is the art target. The playable game is unchanged. The first wave still starts with the original Cannon, 800 HP, zero money and no consumables.
02 / Art pillars

Detail where it matters

01

Readable combat

Clear heads, weapons and vehicle profiles. Quiet terrain under units. Effects communicate hits without hiding threats.

02

Painted character

Chunky military shapes, slightly exaggerated soldiers, soft shadows and tactile materials. Keep the playful arcade personality.

03

Restrained interface

Charcoal surfaces, cream text, amber actions. Green means health; red means danger. No fabric panels or ornate body text.

Motion & sound

Short muzzle flashes and impacts, restrained dust and shake, readable smoke. Cosmetic animation never changes damage timing. Distinct weapons and a quieter rotor mix.

Review before expanding

Validate painted style, character exaggeration, actual-size readability, slot clarity and HUD hierarchy. The remaining campaign art follows the approved playable slice.

# Painted battlefield effects and directed fire

Muzzle effects now use the shot vector, rather than a fixed horizontal direction. Projectile weapons use their launch/path direction, including the rising mortar arc; the first frame derives its direction from the existing trajectory. Barrel-tip origins and helicopter body transforms remain shared with the renderer. Hitscan tracers still run from muzzle to target; explosive weapons no longer draw an instant bullet line alongside their moving shell.

The new `remaster/assets/art/battlefield-vfx-v2.png` is a 4×4 transparent atlas containing four explosion stages, smoke, dust, two directional muzzle flames, a finned mortar shell, a rocket, exhaust, dirt, metal, stone, an ember and barrel smoke. Shell images rotate along the visible path. Explosion stages crossfade, while textured smoke, dust and debris replace the former smooth blobs and square fragments. Damaged/wrecked vehicles also use painted smoke and flame sprites. Supplemental light glows, pressure rings and UI marks remain canvas geometry.

Gameplay damage, radii, projectile update timing, fire rates, progression and original sound recordings remain unchanged. Reduced-motion settings still reduce glow/travel and suppress rocket trails and extra debris.

Validation: 98 tests pass, including asset hash/dimensions, muzzle-to-shot direction, initial mortar tangent, helicopter transforms, and projectile visual endpoints. Empty atlas corners have alpha zero; sampled near-transparent coverage is about 71.7%. Browser inspection checked explosions on terrain, directed defense fire, the mortar launch and the rocket sprite/trail. Screenshots: `textured-battlefield-preview.png`, `textured-muzzle-preview.png`, `textured-rocket-preview.png`. QA uses separate campaign storage. No new performance benchmark or cross-browser certification was performed.

## Asset generation provenance

Used the built-in image generation tool, followed by its built-in image-edit mode for background extraction. The first candidate had pale translucent background haze and was rejected; only the corrected v2 atlas is referenced by the game. Default generated originals remain preserved outside the project. No CLI/API fallback was used.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D war battlefield VFX sprite atlas for a painted military browser game. Create ONE square transparent PNG sprite sheet in a STRICT uniform 4-column by 4-row grid, 16 equally sized cells, no grid lines or text. Each cell contains one separate isolated asset fully inside cell with ample transparent padding, nothing touches neighboring cells. Painterly realistic military effects, textured volumetric detail, organic ragged edges, not flat icons, not vector, not smooth gradient balls. Row 1 left-to-right: four sequential explosion stages: white-hot ignition with orange tongues; expanding turbulent orange fireball with dark smoke fringe; mature billowing orange fire and charcoal smoke; dissipating dense charcoal smoke with tiny glowing pockets. Row 2: billowing isolated charcoal smoke puff; tan earth dust puff; compact rifle muzzle flame pointing RIGHT with origin at left; larger cannon muzzle flame pointing RIGHT with origin at left. Row 3: a detailed olive mortar shell with fins nose pointing RIGHT seen side-on; an olive rocket projectile with metal nose and small tail fins nose pointing RIGHT side-on; short fiery orange rocket exhaust plume trailing LEFT; one irregular rough brown earth clod. Row 4: one jagged dark metal shrapnel fragment; one angular brown stone chunk; small isolated incandescent ember spark; wispy gray barrel smoke puff. No ground plane, no vehicles, no people, no lettering, no checkerboard pattern, no shadows on a background. Genuine transparent alpha everywhere outside objects, preserve natural semi-transparent smoke edges. Crisp readable silhouettes, consistent premium painted battlefield art. Uniform 4x4 layout mandatory.

Final background-extraction prompt:

> Edit target: the provided 4x4 battlefield effects sprite atlas. Background extraction only. Preserve ALL sixteen effect sprites, their cell positions, orientations, colors and scale EXACTLY. Remove ALL white/off-white background and haze between the sprites, including translucent white backdrop, so empty space has alpha exactly zero. Keep organic semitransparent gray and dark smoke and fire edges. This is a game sprite texture: it must composite on dark green terrain without white rectangles or a white veil. True transparent alpha background, not a pale checkerboard or partially white paper. Do not add shadows behind objects. Preserve strict 4x4 layout. No new sprites, no labels, no grid lines.
